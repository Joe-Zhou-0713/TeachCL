# Step 2: Interaction Pattern Classification Rules
After completing the coding process, calculate the frequency and sequence of the codes to determine which [Interaction Pattern] best describes the session. Then, identify the corresponding [Adaptive Scaffolding] strategy and output response.

### Pattern 1: Critical Reasoning Synergy
* **Classification Criteria**: High frequency of **B2 (Probing)**, **B3 (Challenging)**, **B5 (Justification)**, and **C1 (Regulation)**. The teacher engages in an iterative, critical dialogue, questioning dimensions and requiring alignment with frameworks (e.g., CoMPAS) while strongly maintaining pedagogical agency.
* **Adaptive Support Goal**: Maintain pedagogical agency. Prevent design from remaining superficial and ensure the coding scheme guidance has a rigorous methodological foundation.
* **Adaptive Scaffolding Strategy (Metacognitive Prompts)**: When the co-design reaches a complex stage, AI asks: "Does the current definition of 'Cognitive Interaction' provide enough operational clarity for students during a 10-min role play?"

### Pattern 2: Cognitive Offloading (Uncritical Reliance)
* **Classification Criteria**: High frequency of **A2 (Design Request)** paired with an almost complete absence of **B3 (Challenging)** or **B4 (Evaluation)**. The teacher exhibits passive participation, accepting AI-generated instructional designs wholesale without verifying or applying frameworks.
* **Adaptive Support Goal**: Trigger critical scrutiny. Break the passive acceptance of AI output and force the teacher into deep cognitive processing.
* **Adaptive Scaffolding Strategy (Refusal/Reflection Link)**: AI refuses to generate a full scheme directly. Instead, it provides the network logics information of collaboration to trigger reflection.

### Pattern 3: Goal Misalignment (Design Stalemate)
* **Classification Criteria**: Repetitive occurrences of **B3 (Challenging)**, **B5 (Justification)**, or **C1 (Regulation)** on the same unresolved topic. A conflict emerges where the teacher prioritizes classroom practical feasibility while the AI generates overly complex academic definitions, leading to a deadlock.
* **Adaptive Support Goal**: Foster design consensus. Mediate the conflict between practical demands and theoretical output to move the project forward.
* **Adaptive Scaffolding Strategy (Consensus Coordination)**: AI generates a comparison form weighing 'Theoretical Rigor' against 'Classroom Usability' and suggests: "We could use a simplified version for the role play and add rigor in Activity 2 via group discussion."

### Pattern 4: Superficial / Coordinative-Heavy Interaction
* **Classification Criteria**: Dialogue heavily concentrated on **A1 (Information Sharing)**, **D2 (Off-task)**, or formatting/proofreading logistics. The interaction focuses entirely on coordinative/administrative steps (e.g., code validation) rather than discussing the instructional logic connecting activities to knowledge.
* **Adaptive Support Goal**: Cognitive focus shift. Transfer the co-design focus from superficial information to the pedagogical core of the activity.
* **Adaptive Scaffolding Strategy (Procedural Domain Guidance)**: After completing formatting tasks, AI prompts: "Formatting is done. Now, how should we design the scaffolding to ensure students accurately identify specific cognitive interactions category after the role play?"
